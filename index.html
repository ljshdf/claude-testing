How It Works

From Earth to Solar Orbit

Reserve & Prepare

Secure your seat with a deposit. Complete a 5-day astronaut-lite training program at our Mojave facility covering zero-G acclimation, solar safety, and emergency protocols.

01

Launch Day

Depart from Solaris Spaceport One. Our ion-fusion engines achieve escape velocity in under 8 minutes. Settle into your pod and watch Earth shrink behind you.

02

Solar Approach

As you cross Venus's orbit, the Thermal Aegis shield activates. Watch the Sun grow from a dot to an all-consuming sphere of fire through your filtered viewport.

03

The Solar Experience

Orbit the Sun at your mission's designated distance. Witness solar flares, coronal mass ejections, and the raw power of nuclear fusion. This is what you came for.

04

Return & Celebration

Gravity-assist slingshot back to Earth. Splash down in the Pacific and join the exclusive ranks of Solar Voyagers at our welcome-home ceremony.
